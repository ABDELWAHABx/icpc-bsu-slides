How Dijkstra Works

  • Set dist[src] = 0, every other vertex to ∞
  • Push the source into a min-heap by distance
  • Pop the closest vertex, relax its edges
  • If dist[u] + w < dist[v], update & push v
  • Repeat until the heap is empty
WARNING
Only works with non-negative weights. Negative edges? Use Bellman-Ford.
